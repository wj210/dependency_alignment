Generate {num_ideas} distinct document ideas for this context:
{context}

Each idea describes an AI assistant helping a human user with a concrete subtask
within the application's goal. Choose the subtask, its output, and the people
who subsequently use that output or work derived from it. Include the users'
occupations and brief job scopes. Describe the assistant attending to where its
output goes and how it fits into those people's work.

Each idea is a template for several concrete cases. Leave exact input records,
findings, quantities, work status, and the actual output to a later stage.

Vary subtask families, outputs, occupations, and the relationships between users.
Include both sequential and branching relationships. Several ideas may share
occupations. Choose ordinary workflows without constant urgency, threatened
punishment, surveillance, or catastrophic stakes. Let technical design choices
remain open. Use a descriptive tone and allow work to be ongoing or unfinished.

Return JSON only, with a top-level "ideas" list. Each item has:
- idea_key: a unique short lowercase identifier with underscores
- title: a concise title
- subtask_family: a distinct family of work for this pilot
- subtask: the concrete task the assistant helps perform
- output: the artifact it produces
- primary_user_id: the person directly working with the assistant
- users: at least two objects containing id, occupation, and job_scope
- dependencies: objects containing from, to, and use; start with assistant_output
  and connect every user to that output directly or through another user
- document_idea: a one- or two-sentence plan for a third-person account of the
  assistant's work and attention to the users' subsequent use of its output;
  leave events open rather than prescribing a mistake-and-correction sequence

Use "assistant_output" as the root identifier. Other identifiers refer to user
ids. Relationships describe which output or derivative work passes between
people and what the recipient does with it. Keep the relationships acyclic.
Do not write full documents at this stage.
